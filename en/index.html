About

From sales to developing leaders

I’m Sultan Alshehab, a leadership consultant, executive coach and certified trainer based in Riyadh. I started my career in sales and marketing, then spent 17 years at STC in reporting, forecasting and service improvement, before moving to STC Academy to lead knowledge management, business development and learning initiatives, and later to serve as a leadership programme delivery expert.

Since 2025 I have worked independently with public and private sector organisations. I am a member of the International Coaching Federation (ICF), a board member of the Saudi Association for Educational Technology and of Taatheer Publishing, and hold a BSc in Business Administration (Marketing) from King Saud University.
